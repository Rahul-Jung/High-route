Impact

Two things here, and the difference matters. Metrics are the annual figures the Our Impact page publishes. Records are what was actually spent on a departure, which is where the metrics should come from.

Read this before entering a figure

A metric only appears on the public page once it has a value and is marked evidenced. Until then the page shows it as not published, with the note below it explaining what has to be supplied — which is deliberate. The brief this site was built to forbids publishing a sustainability figure nobody measured, so the panel will refuse to let you tick evidenced on a metric with no value, and the gap is visible to visitors rather than hidden.
