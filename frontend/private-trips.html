Private Trips

Every itinerary on this site can be run privately, on dates you choose — and anything that is not on the site can usually be built, because the guides, the permits and the logistics are the same ones we already run.

Tell us what you want

Start the conversation

Answer what you know. "Two weeks in October, four of us, fairly fit, no idea where" is enough to work with — the detail is what the reply is for.

Dietary requirements, medical conditions, accessibility needs, a fixed date you cannot move, someone in the group who is much fitter or much less fit than the rest. The more awkward the constraint, the more useful it is to know now.

Your name and email come from your account, not this form.

What happens next

  1. A guide reads it — not a sales desk — and replies within two working days.
  2. If what you want is sensible, you get an itinerary and a price. If it is not, you get told why and what the nearest workable version is. A fortnight in Manaslu in January is not a trip we will sell you.
  3. Nothing is charged, and nothing is booked, until you have seen the itinerary and the price and said yes.

Private versions of what is already here

Any scheduled trip can be run for your group alone, which mainly changes three things: the dates are yours, the pace is yours, and the group is only you. It usually costs more per person than joining a departure, because the fixed costs are shared between fewer people.
