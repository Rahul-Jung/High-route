Where we go

Destinations

Seven regions, each with a different season, a different altitude and a different reason to go. These pages are geography and permits rather than marketing — including, where it applies, the honest reason we do not run a particular kind of trip somewhere.

Choosing between them

Which region suits which trip

If you have under a week, stay low: the Kathmandu Valley rim and the hills around Pokhara ride all year, need no permits, and do not ask your body to deal with altitude at the same time as jet lag.

If you have ten days or more, the Annapurna and Mustang valleys are the reason people come to Nepal with a bike. Both cross real altitude and both need the acclimatisation days their itineraries build in.

If you want to walk rather than ride, the Khumbu and the Langtang valley are trekking country. The Everest page says plainly why we publish no riding trips there.

If you want to be somewhere quiet, Manaslu is the most remote circuit on this site and the most committing. It is a restricted area, it needs a licensed guide and a minimum group of two, and there is no quick way out of the middle of it.
