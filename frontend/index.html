Choose your riding style

Every tour is tagged to one primary discipline so you know exactly what the days will demand.

1,800–3,200m

Trail

Gradual climbs and flowing, natural descents. The widest range of tours sits here.

2,400–4,200m

Enduro

Steep, technical descents with shuttle or hike-assisted climbs. Built for confident riders.

1,200–2,800m

XC

Endurance-paced cross-country riding through valleys and terraced farmland.

2,800–5,100m

Bikepacking

Multi-day, self-supported expedition riding into Nepal's most remote valleys.

Why ride Nepal with us

Twelve years operating in these mountains taught us what actually matters on a bike here.

Local knowledge

Guides who live here

Every lead guide grew up riding these valleys. They know which line is dry after rain, and which village serves the best dal bhat.

Real logistics

No surprises at altitude

Bike-friendly lodges, workshop stops, and acclimatisation days are built into the itinerary, not left for you to figure out.

Honest grading

You'll know what you're in for

Our five-level difficulty system exists so nobody ends up on a technical descent they didn't sign up for.

Small groups

Six to ten riders, always

Small enough that your guide knows your riding by day two, and can adjust the pace accordingly.

Safety first

A real emergency plan

Every departure carries satellite communication, a wilderness-trained guide, and a mapped evacuation route.

Community-first

Money that stays local

Local guides, local lodges, local trail maintenance — see exactly where your fee goes, further down this page.
