Season by season

When to Visit

October and November are the clearest months and everybody knows it. The useful part of this page is the rest of the year — including which trips work in the monsoon, and why Mustang is the answer.

The short version

Three windows, not two

Autumn — late September to November

The monsoon withdraws, the air is washed clean, and the mountains are visible in a way they are not at any other time of year. It is also the busiest season on the popular trails, and the high passes are reliably open. If you can only come once, come now.

Spring — March to May

Warmer, hazier, and the rhododendron forests below 3,500 m are in flower. The high passes clear through March and April. Afternoons cloud over in the hills, so days start early.

The monsoon — June to September

Most of the country is wet, green and landslide-prone, and most high routes are not sensible. Upper Mustang is the exception, and not a marginal one: it sits in the rain shadow of Annapurna and Dhaulagiri and stays dry and rideable right through. This is the one window where the hardest trip on the site is also the most reliable one.

Winter — December to February

Cold, clear and very quiet. The high passes are closed or closing and many lodges above 3,500 m shut. The Kathmandu Valley rim and the hills around Pokhara, on the other hand, are at their best.
