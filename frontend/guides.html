Why the guide matters more than the itinerary

Every itinerary on this site is a plan, and in the Himalaya a plan meets weather. The person who decides whether to cross a pass today, take the lower line, or sit out a morning is your guide, and that single judgement call matters more to how your trip goes than anything written on a trip page.

It is also why group sizes are capped. A guide who can see everyone can make that call for everyone.

Working conditions

Guides and support team are employed and paid by High Route, and what they are paid is one of the figures the Our Impact page is set up to publish. It is not published yet, because the payroll figures have to come from High Route and we are not going to estimate somebody’s wages.

If this matters to you when you choose an operator — and it should — ask us directly and ask anyone else you are considering the same question.
