  • Check the FAQs first for anything about insurance, equipment, altitude, accommodation or cancellation — those answers are more detailed there than they will be in a reply.

Questions worth asking us

Not a sales list. These are the questions that actually distinguish operators in Nepal, and you should ask them of anyone you are considering, including us:

  • Who guides this trip, and have they led it before?
  • What is the acclimatisation profile, and what happens if I am not acclimatising?
  • What is the 24-hour emergency contact during the trip, and who answers it?
  • What exactly is the evacuation arrangement in this region?
  • What is the deposit, when is the balance due, and what happens if you cancel the departure?
  • If the trip uses porters: what is the weight limit, and are they insured and equipped?

Several of those are things this site cannot yet answer in writing — see what is not documented yet. Ask them anyway, and get the answer before you pay a deposit.
