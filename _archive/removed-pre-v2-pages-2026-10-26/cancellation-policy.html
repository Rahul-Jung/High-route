Cancellation policy

What actually happens, mechanically, when you book, pay, or cancel a departure on this platform. A tour's own page shows any trip-specific terms (for example, non-refundable permit costs on some restricted-area routes) above this baseline.

Securing a place

A booking is confirmed with a 20% deposit. Until that deposit is paid, the booking is held for 72 hours — if no payment is recorded in that window, the hold expires automatically and the seats are released back to that departure, no cancellation request needed on your part.

Paying the balance

The remaining balance is due before departure. You can pay it yourself from your dashboard, in a deposit or full-balance payment, or we can record a payment made another way (bank transfer, cash) on our side.

Cancelling a confirmed booking

You can cancel a not-yet-departed booking yourself from your dashboard, or ask us to. Cancelling marks the booking cancelled and releases its seats immediately. Any refund on what you've already paid is then calculated automatically, based on how many days before departure you cancelled — the exact tiers are configurable and shown to you at the time of cancellation. A refund calculated this way is an entitlement, not a completed payment: it's recorded as "pending" until an admin has actually sent the money back through whatever channel you paid with and confirms it — we don't mark anything "refunded" before that happens. Contact us as soon as you know you need to cancel — the earlier, the larger the refund tier is likely to be.

Tour-specific terms

Some routes carry costs that are genuinely non-refundable on our end once committed — a restricted-area trekking permit applied for weeks in advance is the most common example. Where that applies to a specific tour, it's stated on that tour's own page. If a tour's page doesn't mention a specific term, we haven't set one — that isn't a promise either way, so ask us if it matters to your decision.

If a departure doesn't happen

If we cancel a scheduled departure (for example, not enough riders for a request-only date), you're not charged, and any payment already made is fully refunded.
